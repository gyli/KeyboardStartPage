# Keyboard Start Page Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Keyboard Start Page does not collect, track, or sell any data.

**What is stored.** Your links and settings are saved with Chrome's sync storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them to your other devices. A wallpaper you choose is saved on your device only. Nothing is ever sent to the developer.

**Website icons.** To show each site's icon, the extension asks Google's public favicon service (`www.google.com/s2/favicons`) for the icon of the site's domain, for example `youtube.com`. Only the domain is sent, not the full address. Icons are cached on your device for 30 days. This request is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Permissions.** `storage` saves your links and settings. `activeTab` lets the toolbar popup read the address of the page you're on, only when you open the popup, so it can offer to add that page to a key.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/gyli/KeyboardStartPage/issues.
