Keyboard Start Page

Help

  • Press a key on the new tab page to open its link, or click the key.
  • Click an empty key, or drop a link on it, to add a link. Hover a key and click the pencil to change it.
  • Chrome puts the cursor in the address bar on new tabs. If keys don’t respond, press Tab first, or turn on “Focus the page on new tabs” below.
  • Click the extension’s icon in the toolbar to open the keyboard over any page.
