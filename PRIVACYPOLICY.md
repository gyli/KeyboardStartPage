# Keyboard Start Page Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Keyboard Start Page does not collect, track, or sell any data.

**What is stored.** Your links and settings are saved with Chrome's sync storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them to your other devices. They are never sent to the developer.

**Website icons.** To show each site's icon, the extension asks Google's public favicon service (`www.google.com/s2/favicons`) for the icon of the site's domain, for example `youtube.com`. Only the domain is sent, not the full address. This request is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Permissions.** The extension only asks for the `storage` permission, to save your links and settings.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/gyli/KeyboardStartPage/issues.
